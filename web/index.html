Privacy: Data processing happens entirely in this browser tab. Files are not uploaded, stored, or transmitted. This page does not load external scripts or fonts.

Interpretation: DeLong standard errors require at least two positive and two negative observations. The paired comparison assumes both scores were measured on the same subjects, in matching CSV rows. It does not adjust for cutpoint optimization, clustered data, or multiple testing. AUC confidence intervals are clipped to [0, 1].
